What this proof establishes

The artwork has live English and Arabic type, the approved vector mark, and the original illustration pixels. The bottle view projects that same artwork around a cylinder.

Ø29 × 108 mm and 30 ml come from the owner's brief. The 108 mm height datum is unresolved unless entered above. Cap, shoulder and the two 32 × 60 mm decoration areas are proposals. Clear glass and chrome describe materials; they are not print inks.

The accepted concept IS the design. The execution must match its composition, colours, full-body swoosh and every line of lettering. Amber is fill through clear glass.
