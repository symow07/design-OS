## Claims

1. `t=0:52` (frame) Grey chipboard panels are positioned by hand onto a large
   white wrapped sheet on a pegboard/vacuum table — separate boards per panel with
   visible hinge gaps between them, i.e. a collapsible rigid box is multiple boards
   mounted on one continuous wrap that acts as the hinge.
2. `t=1:18` (frame) The wrapped panel assembly (white boards, red wrap with turned
   edges) is handled as a flat multi-panel sheet by two workers — at 750mm scale
   this is a two-person operation.
3. `t=1:44` (frame) Finished wrapped flat shells are stacked in piles at a bench —
   the product ships and stores FLAT; erection happens later (or at the customer).
4. `t=2:08` (frame + on-screen caption "Assemble the folding box") Assembly of the
   flattened shell is manual: worker peels release liners off pre-applied adhesive
   zones (triangular corner gussets visible) and uses small wooden press blocks;
   small dark rectangular inserts (magnets) sit in dedicated slots.
5. `t=2:41` (frame) The flat shell erects into a deep rectangular box by folding
   walls up; corner geometry self-locates — the same worker erects it alone in
   seconds, no tools.
6. `t=0:08`, `t=2:08` (frames) Corner gussets are pre-glued triangular flaps with
   peel-off liners — the folding-carton "crash-lock corner" idea executed in
   adhesive on a rigid box.
7. `t=3:08` (outro frame) Finished lid shows a blind-debossed logo ("PAWIS") on
   the wrap — decoration is applied to the wrap, consistent with the wrap-first
   construction in claim 1.

## Engine hooks

- Rigid-box archetype split: RIGID ≠ one construction. Collapsible/magnetic
  folding boxes (this note) fold flat with a continuous outer wrap as hinge;
  classic set-up boxes (see rigid-box gluing note when captured) are erected and
  stay erected. Engine box-format vocabulary should carry `rigid_setup` vs
  `rigid_collapsible` — different dieline logic, different freight cost story.
- Claim 3 is the commercial reason this format exists: flat shipping. Relevant to
  any luxury-carton recommendation the OS makes where freight/storage matters
  (cross-link luxury_teardowns iPhone-style note on magnetic closures).
- Claim 1 means the WRAP carries the artwork and the hinge: printable surface is
  one continuous sheet — artwork for a collapsible rigid box is closer to a
  folding-carton dieline (panels + gaps) than to set-up-box panel wraps.
- Claim 4/6: magnet slots and pre-glued gussets are converter-side details; the
  OS's dieline emitter should NOT attempt to spec them — supplier-owned geometry
  (aligns with "manufacturer supplies the dieline", Lumi note claim 4).
