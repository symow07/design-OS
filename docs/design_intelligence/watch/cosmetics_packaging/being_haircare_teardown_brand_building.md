## Claims

1. [t=0:50–1:25 + frame 0:55] Luxury restraint on the outer: silver mylar wrap, ONE
   deep deboss logo, one line of white text — nothing else. Deboss applied AFTER
   wrapping so the impression compresses board + mylar together, not just paper.
2. [t=1:06] Ink chemistry follows substrate: on mylar use offset UV ink (fast cure);
   conventional inks dry slowly on plastic film.
3. [t=1:45–1:56] Closure engineering is width-driven: 22-inch lid needed THREE magnets;
   two would pop loose and read as cheap. Perceived quality lives in the close.
4. [t=3:30–4:00] Storytelling brevity rule: interior copy in ~5 quick chunks (claim
   line, no-list, URL, socials, one testimonial). Too much story → skimmed → connection
   lost. Copy must not delay reaching the product.
5. [t=2:49–3:20] Inclusive positioning device: "for being you / all hair types" —
   copy addresses the person, not a hair-type segment.
6. [t=5:36–6:20 + frame 5:50] Custom-molded jar: brand name debossed INTO the mold
   (matte-vs-gloss contrast), one-hand-openable lip lid; shelf differentiation via a
   blue/green pair against a category of whites/ivories/pale blues. Decoration silk-
   screened direct-to-pack.
7. [t=9:01–9:41 + frame 9:15] Form novelty as hero: shampoo tubes with inverted
   proportions (short, fat, stands on end) — familiar format, alien proportions reads
   as new. Molded icon language on front (equal-sign / dots) differentiates SKUs but
   [t=10:03] undeciphered icons are a real legibility risk the reviewer flags.
8. [t=12:30–14:35 + frame 13:25] Cost anatomy of a rigid kit: 2mm board; mylar wrap
   is a major cost over paper; insert = cross-cut board panels (die blade partway
   through, fold to a ledge — fast/cheap) vs V-groove edges (sharp corners, slower,
   costs more); corner tape by machine, spot glue by hand.
9. [t=15:40–16:20] Process rule: always ask the manufacturer WHY a construction choice
   was made — surfaces cost savings and catches unexamined defaults.
10. [t=16:41–17:22] Influencer-kit balance: oversize footprint buys presence but
    product protection needs only modest clearance; dimensions can be shaved without
    losing the monolithic feel. Un-glued interior covers become instant waste — glue
    them in [t=4:33–4:55].
