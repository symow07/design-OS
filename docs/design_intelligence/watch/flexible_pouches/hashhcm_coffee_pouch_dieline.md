## Engine hooks

- Claim 2 gives the corpus's first pouch dimension anchor: 250g coffee ≈
  140 mm wide × 80 mm gusset (height ~240 mm per the 0:31 dialog, low
  confidence). Usable as a sanity-check ratio (gusset ≈ 55–60% of width), not
  a spec.
- Claims 3–4: pouch dielines carry CURVED seal geometry (gusset arcs) unlike
  carton dielines' straight creases — the M7 emitter's line vocabulary
  (cut/crease/bleed) needs a `seal` line class for pouch formats, and seal
  zones double as the corner keep-outs PackMojo's note names.
- Claim 4's layer separation (dieline layer vs linked artwork file) matches the
  Lumi/prepress convention: dieline is its own locked layer, artwork links in.
- Claim 5 reinforces the standing rule: even tutorial authors start from a
  supplier's pouch dieline — the engine should never generate pouch seal
  geometry from scratch.
