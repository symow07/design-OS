## Claims

1. `t=0:04`–`t=0:15` Corrugated board = a fluted (wavy) paper sandwiched between
   a **top liner** and a **bottom liner** — liner/flute/liner is the named
   construction.
2. `t=0:15`–`t=0:31` **Ply counting**: "a three-ply board refers to two plain
   paper plus one corrugated paper" — ply = total sheet count, liners included.
3. `t=0:31`–`t=0:39` **5-ply** = 3-ply + another flute layer + another liner
   (i.e., double-wall in Western vocabulary).
4. `t=0:57`–`t=1:14` Weight rule of thumb: product weight **15–20 kg → 3-ply**
   box; "anything more than this we may need to go for a five ply box."
5. `t=1:14`–`t=1:20` Caveat stated in-video: the weight rule is "just a rule of
   thumb — there are few other factors like GSM [and] flute design."
