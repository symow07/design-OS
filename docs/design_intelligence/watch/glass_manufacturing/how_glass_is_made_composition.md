## Claims

1. [t=0:22–0:45] Recipe ratios: silica sand ~45% (backbone), soda ash ~15%
   (evens melting), limestone ~10% (durability), plus cullet (recycled glass)
   integrated seamlessly.
2. [t=1:06] Melt: 24-hour cycle at ~1500°C yields molten glass "the
   consistency of honey," cut into gobs at precise intervals.
3. [t=1:29–1:50] Forming: gob → preliminary mold → parison (miniature of the
   final bottle) → blow mold with compressed air.
4. [t=1:50–2:10] Color chemistry: colored bottles add small amounts of iron,
   sulfur, and carbon to the mix — color is a MELT decision, not a coating.
5. [t=2:10] Molded relief branding: a mold with a recessed insignia yields a
   RAISED design on the bottle surface — embossed glass branding comes from
   tooling, at forming time.
6. [t=2:10–2:50] Thermal + QC chain: flame treatment prevents thermal-shock
   cracks; annealing lehr cools at a controlled rate; automatic camera/probe
   inspection covers dimensions and THREAD INTEGRITY for screw caps, then
   visual inspection.
7. [t=2:50–3:10] Cullet economics: up to 90% of the mix can be cullet; it
   melts at lower temperature — ~2.5% energy saving per 10% cullet used.

## Engine hooks

- Claim 4 vs the decoration matrix → TWO routes to colored glass: in-melt
  color (iron/sulfur/carbon — permanent, volume-gated, whole-batch) vs wave-2
  surface routes (spray coating, frosting — per-order). The engine's bottle
  spec should distinguish melt-color from applied-color; small-batch BBROSE
  work almost certainly lands on applied color.
- Claim 5 → embossed logos on glass are a TOOLING line item (custom mold),
  not a decoration line item — different cost class and lead time than
  silkscreen; connects to the emboss/deboss note's die logic, here in glass.
- Claim 7 → quantified sustainability lever: high-cullet glass has a real
  energy story (~2.5%/10% cullet) — usable in brand-facing sustainability
  copy IF the supplier confirms cullet fraction.
- Claim 6 → thread integrity is an inspected dimension — corroborates the
  neck-finish note: the finish is precision geometry, machine-checked.
