## Claims

1. [t=0:07–0:29] Scope: silkscreen prints logos/images/words DIRECTLY onto
   containers, tubes, and caps — direct decoration, no label.
2. [t=0:29] Color separation constraint: each color in the artwork is extracted to
   its own acetate film — one screen pass per color.
3. [t=0:50–1:32] Stencil creation: light-sensitive emulsion coated evenly on the
   screen → exposed through the film (light hardens open areas; design areas stay
   liquid) → unhardened emulsion rinsed out to form the stencil. Exposure time
   varies with emulsion thickness, light intensity, screen size.
4. [t=1:53–2:13] Adhesion pretreatment: PE and PP containers require flame
   treatment (or similar surface treatment) before printing or ink won't adhere —
   substrate chemistry gates the decoration.
5. [t=3:06–3:27] Drying regimes differ by material: plastics air-dry; glass is
   oven-dried at high temperature; UV inks cure instantly under UV. A new color
   can only print after the previous one is dry — colors are serialized.
