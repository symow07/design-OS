## Engine hooks

- Claim 3 is the one that matters for BBROSE: fully recyclable PUMPS now
  exist. The airless/treatment-pump components in jars_closures are
  classically multi-material (metal spring + mixed plastics = landfill);
  a mono-material pump makes a full BE-sustainable dispensing SKU possible
  — a sourcing question to add to the supplier templates.
- Claims 2, 4 → the industry pattern: mono-material variants are marketed
  by matching legacy performance specs (puncture, oxygen barrier) — the
  Nosco foil-core trade-off (barrier vs recyclability) is being engineered
  away; the material picker's trade-off table needs a date/state field, not
  a fixed rule.
- Overall → vendor-name inventory (Gualapack, ProAmpac, TriMas, Ahlstrom,
  WALKI) seeds a mono-material supplier shortlist alongside the US
  bottle/jar supplier note.
