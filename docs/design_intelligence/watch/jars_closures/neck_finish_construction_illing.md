## Claims

1. [t=0:07–0:27] Sealing anatomy: the FINISH is the bottle lip; the LAND AREA is
   the lip portion that contacts the closure's gasket/liner to form the seal —
   the seal happens at land-to-liner contact, not on the threads.
2. [t=0:27–0:47] Thread + transfer bead: CT = continuous thread closure; the
   transfer bead (ring below the neck) is a molding artifact that can be
   modified for tamper evidence.
3. [t=0:47–1:28] Dimension system: T = outside diameter of threads (measure
   outermost threads, in mm); E = outside diameter of neck; I = inner diameter;
   (E−T)/2 = thread depth.
4. [t=1:08–1:48] Finish-series code from thread turns: thread passes itself
   once = 400 (glass: 405); 1.5 turns = 410; tall neck 2 turns = 415; short
   neck 2 turns = 425; Y-threads = 430; lug = 2000. T-dimension + series =
   the neck finish spec (e.g. 20-410).
5. [t=1:48–2:09] I-dimension constraints: minimum I needed for filling-tube
   clearance; linerless closures with plug/land seals and dispensing
   plugs/fitments require a CONTROLLED I for proper fit.
6. [t=2:09–2:30] S dimension (top of finish → top edge of first thread) sets
   closure ORIENTATION and thread engagement; H = height of the finish.
7. [t=2:30–3:11] Body anatomy: label area, heel, base with PUSH-UP (concave —
   stability, reduces interior volume, carries recycling code + cavity number +
   label-registration feature); standard capacity fills to shoulder, overflow
   capacity to the very top.
