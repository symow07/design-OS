## Claims

1. [t=0:00–0:20] Architecture: follower-piston airless system = NO dip tube;
   pump core dispenses a measured dose per stroke; one-way valves control flow
   direction; pump resets/refills on release.
2. [t=0:20–0:42] The airless part: as product depletes, a follower piston rises
   to push formula toward the pump, keeping the product chamber tight —
   consistent dosing first pump to last.
3. [t=0:42] Venting: a small vent path at the base lets air in BELOW the piston
   to balance pressure; air stays separated from the formula.
4. [t=0:42–1:03] Selection rule: pump core and piston fit must match the
   formula's VISCOSITY and the FILL METHOD — airless is not
   formula-agnostic.
