## Engine hooks

- Claims 1, 3, 4, 5 → finish-register map for the label spec: glossy = mainstream
  retail; matte/uncoated paper = craft; kraft = artisan/natural; clear = modern
  no-label-look. Complements the Label Apeel taxonomy note (same four registers,
  here shown on physical samples) — safe to encode as a finish→register enum.
- Claim 5 → no-label look is a distinct concept device for BBROSE glass bottles:
  clear film + artwork reads as direct-on-glass decoration at label cost — a
  cheap alternative to the silkscreen/decal routes in the bottle_decoration
  notes; add as an option in the decoration decision matrix.
- Claim 6 → hand-application tolerance: small-batch labels get applied by hand on
  curves — dielines should avoid full-wrap registration-critical seams at low
  volumes.
