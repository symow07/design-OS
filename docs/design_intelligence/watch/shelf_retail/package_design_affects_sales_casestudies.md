## Claims

1. [t=0:43–1:24] Measurement exists: eye-tracking studies (glasses-based,
   in-store, concepts shelved NEXT TO competition) record what shoppers look
   at first, time-to-find, and dwell time — aggregated over hundreds of
   participants to replace gut feeling on "million-dollar" packaging tweaks.
2. [t=1:44–2:25] RXBAR case (redesign win): same product, new package —
   $2M sales (2014, original) → $160M (2017, redesign); the flat
   ingredients-as-hero design is credited.
3. [t=2:25–3:07] Chobani case (repositioning win): market leader becoming
   indistinguishable on shelf; redesign went muted colors + rustic font +
   hand-drawn images to read artisan/natural/premium and recover shelf
   draw.
4. [t=3:07–3:49] Tropicana case (redesign failure): 2009 dropped the iconic
   orange-with-straw for a glass-of-juice image; sales fell ~20% in two
   months; consensus post-mortems: mixed messaging vs old equity, and the new
   graphics read CHEAPER than before.
5. [t=1:24–1:44, 3:49] Land O'Lakes framing: icon-removal redesigns are
   equity gambles whose outcome only time shows — presented as an open case.
