## Claims

1. [t=0:20–1:01] Material menu: LDPE + HDPE standard; also PCR (post-consumer
   resin), sugarcane bioplastic, aluminium, and EVOH (barrier layer) — resin +
   masterbatch color go into the extruder together.
2. [t=1:01–1:21] Process order: extrude → cool → cut to length (hollow sleeve,
   open both ends) → injection-mold the SHOULDER (the part the cap screws onto)
   and weld it to the sleeve → then print.
3. [t=1:21–2:04] Decoration menu on formed tubes: silkscreen, offset, hot
   stamping (gold/silver/colored foils), and labeling — UV ink printed directly
   onto the tube.
4. [t=2:04–2:46] PBL/ABL exception (plastic/aluminium barrier laminate): the
   laminate web is printed FLAT before being formed into a tube — different
   machinery, but yields highly detailed print on a round surface.
5. [t=2:25–2:46] Caps: flip-top caps injection-molded separately, machine-closed,
   screwed onto tubes.
6. [t=2:46–3:26] QC: high-speed camera checks artwork against the tube; human
   secondary check on machine rejects.
7. [t=3:26–3:47] Lead times: ~6 weeks order-to-finish; 6–8 weeks with shipping;
   ~10 weeks total with sea shipment. Tubes ship EMPTY to the customer/filler,
   who fills and crimps.
