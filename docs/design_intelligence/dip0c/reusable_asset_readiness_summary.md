# Reusable asset readiness — DIP-0C1/C2

**`PILOT_VALIDATED`: ~24 of 2,305 files (1.0%).
~2,281 remain unaudited at corpus scale.**

This is the largest gap in DIP-0, and it is stated plainly rather than buried.

## What the pilot established

Technical readiness (axis A) — `TECHNICALLY_READY`, `TECHNICALLY_USABLE_WITH_CAVEATS`,
`NEEDS_CLEANUP`, `NEEDS_VECTOR_RECONSTRUCTION`, `REFERENCE_ONLY`, `QUARANTINE`,
`REJECT_CANDIDATE`, `REQUIRES_TOOL`, `HEADER_ONLY_PARTIAL` — and rights (axis B) are
**separate axes that never collapse into one score**.

AssetCandidate segmentation, pattern seamlessness, perceptual duplicates, colourway and
cross-format families, canonical candidates: all built, all tested, all proven on a frozen
pilot of 24 files.

## What remains

**~2,281 reusable-material files have never been deeply audited.** Running C1/C2 at corpus
scale is the **Reusable Asset Corpus Expansion** workstream. It is planned work, not a
hidden omission.

## Before any reusable asset may be retrieved

- `asset_type_human_confirmed`
- `technical_readiness_accepted`
- `ownership_and_licence_reviewed`
- `retrieval_explicitly_granted`
- `scope_recorded_brand_product_market_media`
- `restrictions_and_expiry_recorded`

Default: **`retrieval_allowed = false`**.
