# DIP-0A — Decisions of record

Approved 2026-07-10. These decisions govern DIP-0A and constrain every later
Design Intelligence phase. Where a decision differs from an earlier estimate,
the decision here supersedes it.

## 0. Source-folder rules (standing)

`materials/` is **read-only source material**. Nothing in it is renamed, moved,
overwritten, optimised, converted, deduplicated or deleted — including junk
files. Every generated artifact lives outside it, under
`var/design_intelligence/audits/dip0/`. `tools/dip0/config.assert_output_safe()`
refuses any write resolving inside the source root, and each run proves the
invariant with a pre/post snapshot plus a hash re-verification.

## 1. `COLOR_PALETTE` — approved

A supported knowledge category for `.ase` Adobe Swatch Exchange files, future
palette files, approved colour-system documents, Pantone references and brand
colour collections (`config.PALETTE_EXT`).

For `Pantone_3519C.ase`: `category = COLOR_PALETTE`, `parser_support = partial`,
`review_required = true`, `automatic_use_allowed = false` until parsed and
human-approved, `retrieval_allowed = false` until DIP-0C/DIP-1 establishes the
palette ingestion contract.

The filename alone never confirms an official BBROSE palette — this file's
`ownership_status` is `UNKNOWN`, and the rule records that caveat as evidence.
The extension rule is evaluated **before** folder hints, so a swatch inside a
`*-background/` folder cannot be misfiled as a `BACKGROUND`.

## 2. `SUPPORTING_METADATA` — approved

A general inventory category for provenance / management sidecars, with a
subtype. `Fonts/fms_metadata.xml` → `category_subtype = FONT_METADATA`,
`associated_branch = materials/Fonts`.

It stays inventoried and traceable, never enters design retrieval, is never
treated as a reusable asset, and remains available as font-management or
provenance evidence.

This keeps `UNKNOWN` reserved for genuinely unidentified files. **UNKNOWN is now
zero.**

## 3. `ARCHIVE` — approved as an inventory/source category, *not* a knowledge class

An archive is a transport/storage container. It is inventoried, hashed and
manifest-inspected; expanded size and archive relationships are reported. It is
excluded from retrieval and generation by rule, never auto-extracted, and its
existence is **not** proof that its contents are licensed or approved
(`review_reasons` carries `archive_contents_unverified`).

Archive contents are deferred to a future controlled extraction milestone.

## 4. `LICENSE_EVIDENCE` — approved as an evidence category, *not* a knowledge class

Licence/readme/EULA/terms material is linked to nearby assets and fonts as
supporting evidence via nearest-ancestor folder association. Records keep
`license_evidence[]` (source path, scope, distance, strength),
`license_evidence_strength`, association method, confidence and the untouched
`human_confirmed` block.

**Its existence never changes licence status automatically.** `license_status`
only ever holds `UNVERIFIED` or `PENDING_HUMAN_CONFIRMATION`; the inferred
confidence on that field is pinned to `0.0`, because evidence is not proof. No
asset becomes commercially approved because a licence file sits beside it.

## 5. `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — kept, with a review sheet

Category and exclusion behaviour unchanged. `OUT_OF_SCOPE` means *we understand
what the file is and deliberately exclude it*; `UNKNOWN` means *the system
cannot yet classify it*. The two never merge.

`reports/out_of_scope_review.csv` (48 rows) lists relative path, filename,
detected type, size, exclusion rule, evidence, confidence, recommended
disposition and a blank human-confirmed field. Format-driven exclusions
(confidence ≥ 0.8 — `.mp4`, `.xlsx`) are recommended `exclude_permanently`;
rule-inferred ones (apparel/bathroom mockups) are recommended
`human_confirm_exclusion`. Nothing is deleted, moved or modified.

## 6. Font review scale — recorded as a DIP-0C requirement

7,361 files requiring review is technically correct but operationally wrong:
human review must not demand one decision per font file. Recorded in
`known_limitations.md` as a DIP-0C requirement — font review must be groupable by
source folder, family, version, duplicate group, licence-evidence group and
quarantine status, allowing a reviewer to approve or reject a family/source batch
while retaining file-level provenance. **Not implemented in DIP-0A.**

## 7. Milestone records

Heavy artifacts (full 7,586-row inventory, previews, caches, large duplicate
reports) stay under the git-ignored `var/design_intelligence/audits/dip0/`. This
directory holds the permanent, committed record: `summary.md`, `decisions.md`,
`inventory_schema.md`, `pilot_manifest.json`, `known_limitations.md`.

`summary.md` and `pilot_manifest.json` are regenerated by
`python -m tools.dip0.run --milestone`, so the record is reproducible rather
than hand-maintained.

### The `.zip` counting error (documented transparently)

The pre-audit reported **~18 GB of ZIP archives** and an "effective library of
~10 GB". Both figures were **wrong**.

A *directory* in the library is literally named `25.10.15.zip`. The pre-audit
command `find materials -name "*.zip" -exec du -ch {} +` therefore matched that
**directory**, and `du` recursed into it, double-counting the two 3.57 GB
archives stored inside (`25.10.15.zip/25.10.15.zip` and
`25.10.15.zip/25.10.15 (1).zip`). The 7.14 GB of double-counting accounts for
almost exactly the 18 GB − 11.69 GB discrepancy.

Confirmed by `stat` and by the audit itself: **archives total 11.69 GB**,
expanding to ~15.42 GB. Non-archive material is therefore ~18.2 GB, not ~10 GB.

## 9. Ownership

Folder location never establishes ownership. `BBROSE.pdf` and `BBROSE-1.pdf` are
`BRAND_MASTER` / `LIKELY_BBROSE_UNCONFIRMED`; `logo.pdf` and `LOGO6.ai` are
`LOGO` / `LIKELY_BBROSE_UNCONFIRMED`. All four still require human confirmation.

`ATC LOGO.ai` and `JOJIA LOGO.ai` sit in the same folder and are
`UNCONFIRMED_THIRD_PARTY` with `excluded_from_brand_retrieval = true`.

A brand **master** is a vector/PDF original. Twelve AI-generated product-render
PNGs carrying "BBROSE" in the filename are `PRODUCT_REFERENCE`, not
`BRAND_MASTER` — a filename must never promote a render into brand retrieval.
