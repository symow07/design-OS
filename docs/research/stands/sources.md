**Spot-checked by the executor against the pages themselves, before any number went into a rule:** FEFCO Rec. 101 (read from the PDF: inside length and breadth are crease centres less one thickness), US10683131 (a header clamped on the wall's top edge by a tongue, no glue), US10144549 (three-ply sides, tabs trapped between a wall's leaves), US10159361 (rolled double walls, a shelf sloping to the rear, E, B or C flute), Cito's double crease (3–8 mm centres, for a 180° fold in corrugated). All five said what the research said they said.

Three more were spot-checked from the best-in-class file, and two of its lines overreached. **D1 (Nivea):** the jury's stated reason is brand coherence; the wave-shaped pedestal is the maker's description, not what the judges named. **D2 (Revlon):** the page says a two-part body and "a triangular base panel for insertion"; it does not say the panel locks it. **D5's** brief holds, as a goal: the buyer wanted "ideally" no fluting, raw edges, polyester fill or plastic tape; the page does not confirm they were removed. No engine rule rests on these lines; the raw file carries the corrections.

**What the web did not settle.** The research says so plainly: no code for a tray with a header; no tab, slot, web or platform numbers for corrugated; no tear pattern for corrugated; no retailer's tip test; no assembly-time target. Each such rule in `rules/_common.json` is graded X and marked provisional, and it prints as ⟪PROVISIONAL⟫ until a folded sample decides it.

**One source to discount.** S21 in the counter file is a search-results link, not a page. The file says so; nothing rests on it.

**Part 3's research (2026-10-31).** Spot-checked by the executor against the pages: US4753341 (E16: an inner rear panel folded over the wall panels face to face; without its flaps "a raw (cut) edge" shows) and US10470593 (E1: a tester unit "in front of, and optionally adjacent to and abutting a merchandising tray"; a product 20–80 % of the distance between peaks; a front surface 20–90°). **What the web did not settle:** no board tester well, ledge or slot with dimensions; no tester clearance; no tester step height; no tilt for board; no wipe data for a coating. Every tester holder with numbers is plastic, acrylic or wire. The house's own dies (house-references.md) gave more than the web did. Grades as above; each source is listed in its file.
