# PROGRAMME FINDING — MEASUREMENT ADEQUACY (2026-08-08)

Recorded at the programme level on the owner's instruction, unsoftened.

THE FINDING. The palette characterization protocol compared measured
statistics against thresholds and committed verdicts on those
comparisons without measuring the reproducibility of most of the
statistics involved, and did not notice until an adequacy
precondition (1i) was written for an unrelated reason — the
near-ceiling 1a validity reading of the counted-instrument
micronormal sweep.

HOW LONG. By commit dates: from the first committed threshold verdict
(M5 reflective metric report, 2026-08-07, commit 294a98f) to 1i
(2026-08-08, commit 8f6cb81). The owner's brief said "months"; the
record says the protocol's ENTIRE verdict-producing history — one
compressed working span covering two protocol generations. The force
of the finding is structural, not durational: every threshold verdict
the protocol ever produced was affected. The calendar correction is
recorded because softening in either direction is the failure mode
this file exists to prevent.

HOW MANY. 31 committed threshold comparisons inventoried. At the
retroactive sweep: 4 adequate, 1 ADEQUACY_PRECONDITION_UNMET, 26
never assessed. After analytic discharge (A11) and the cheap direct
measurements: 11 established (10 + the founding comparison resolved
ESTABLISHED_DEGENERATE), 3 ADEQUACY_PRECONDITION_UNMET, 11
NOT_ESTABLISHED_PENDING_SPREAD, 6 standing by the 1j burden rule
(exclusions, a not-certified FAIL, a degeneracy).

THE PARTIAL PROTECTION, in the same paragraph and with equal weight:
A11 held where the owner argued it — R4's separation margin
(clearing >= 2 x max seed-only floor) was structurally enforcing the
adequacy condition for every floor-limited clearing comparison all
along, under another name and unnamed. Six comparisons discharged
analytically from committed records alone, and three more were
honestly converted to UNMET by the same spreads. The protection was
partial and unnamed, not absent. The good news does not soften the
finding; the finding does not suppress the good news.

WHAT NOW PREVENTS RECURRENCE. 1i and 1j are hashed into the protocol
identity (3eb1a9b0fe36bfe4): every threshold comparison now carries
an explicit adequacy precondition, non-established affirmative
verdicts suspend rather than operate, and the establishment table
(docs/roadmap/palt_m6/establishment_closure/establishment_table.json)
is the single consultation point for every downstream node — verdict
records are no longer consulted directly. The degenerate-statistic
guard (1g) makes unguarded rank correlations a test failure. What
remains open is enumerated with named completing nodes and costs:
M6-DECLARED-ENSEMBLE-SPREADS (~54 renders), M6-CENSUS-REPLICATE
(~32-48 renders), M6-FLOOR-STATISTIC-REPLICATE (expensive).
