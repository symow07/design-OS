# PALT-M4 — honest visual assessment (not self-approval)

Assessed against the owner's §9 standard. **I am not approving this
geometry**; this is my own harsh read of the neutral clay evidence.

## Meets the standard

- **Controlled edge radii** — 6.0 mm corner radius plus 1.2 mm top/bottom
  edge fillets on both shells; the palette reads as moulded plastic, not a
  crude box.
- **Coherent wall thickness** — 2.0 mm case wall throughout, locally
  thickened to 3.2 mm at the hinge and 3.0 mm at the clasp, which is the
  PALT-M3 envelope applied rather than the mechanism shrunk to fit.
- **Balanced outer proportions** — square 116 × 116 × 15 with a visible
  base/lid split.
- **Clean pan spacing** — nine wells on a resolved 30.6 mm pitch with
  4.0 mm gaps; the grid reads evenly.
- **Correctly seated mirror** — recessed inside a 2.0 mm lid border and
  retained by its frame.
- **Credible hinge and clasp placement** — three knuckles along the top
  edge, a clasp at the bottom edge, both inside their thickened walls.
- **Clean parting line** — a single 0.2 mm parting gap runs unbroken
  around the case.
- **No floating components; no arbitrary gaps** — every component is
  resolved from the contracts, and all 19 validators pass.

## Weaknesses I am reporting rather than hiding

1. **The section view is not a true section.** It is a deterministic
   component clip (half the components hidden), so it reads almost
   identically to the closed side view. A real cut would need CSG the
   builder does not have. Labelled honestly on the image.
2. **The clasp close-up is weak evidence.** At 16 × 2.2 × 2.6 mm the
   clasp reads as a fine line at this crop; the detail is present but not
   legible enough to judge the mechanism.
3. **The hinge close-up is only adequate.** The barrels are visible but
   sit flush in the wall, so the knuckle rhythm is subtle.
4. **Pan wells read as shallow.** At 3.5 mm deep in a 116 mm case the
   wells are correct but visually understated; a deeper 5.0 mm pan family
   would read more premium (a real choice, not a defect).
5. **No draft angles are modelled.** Every wall is vertical. Real ABS
   tooling needs draft, which will slightly change every surface.
6. **The mirror is a flat solid with no attachment detail** — no adhesive
   layer or retention lip is modelled.
