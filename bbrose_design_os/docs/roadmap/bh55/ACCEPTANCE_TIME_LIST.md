# THE ACCEPTANCE-TIME LIST — §BH55 clause 3

> "INSTRUMENTS ARE FROZEN. No band, threshold, margin, ceiling, unit, axis or rule is touched unless a design I am
> actually trying to accept is refused by it. Every open question of that kind goes to one ACCEPTANCE-TIME list,
> untouched. … They are real and they keep." — OWNER STANDING ORDER §BH55, clause 3 (OWNER_BLOCKER_CLEARANCE.md, §BH55)

ONE list. An entry is ADDED when work notices an instrument question; it is never acted on from here. An entry LEAVES
only (a) when a design the owner is actually trying to accept is refused by it — then it is repaired, in its own
commit, and the entry says so — or (b) in Phase 4 of §BH55, "THE QUEUE, EMPTIED". Each entry: what, where it lives,
what was measured, when it was listed.

> **CORRECTION, 2026-09-20 — §BH55 Phase 4. CLAUSE (b) ABOVE IS OURS AND IT CONTRADICTS HIS ORDER.** The paragraph
> is left exactly as it was written (§AZ0(u)); this is the correction under it. Clause (a) is his sentence. Clause
> (b) is not in §BH55 and never was: clause 3 reads "Every open question of that kind goes to one ACCEPTANCE-TIME
> list, **untouched** … **They are real and they keep**", and it gives exactly ONE release — a design he is actually
> trying to accept being refused by the instrument. Phase 4 names this list among the items to empty, and what that
> can mean without contradicting clause 3 is what this phase did: **make the list true and complete, not resolve
> it**. So no entry left in Phase 4, and reading (b) as a door would have unfrozen, by our own paragraph, the
> instruments his own paragraph froze.
