SUBJECT: 30 ml cylindrical frosted-glass serum bottle + dropper closure — technical specification and decoration request
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hello,

We are specifying packaging for a 30 ml cylindrical frosted-glass cosmetic serum bottle with a metallic collar and dropper closure. We need manufacturer specification data rather than marketing copy, for one exact product variant that you can actually supply.

CONTAINER — please send:
  - exact supplier and product code
  - bottle technical drawing (CAD or dimensioned PDF)
  - nominal capacity and overflow capacity
  - body diameter, overall height, shoulder height
  - neck finish, neck outer diameter, neck inner diameter
  - glass weight and any wall or construction notes
  - dimensional tolerances
  - frosting/coating capability and colour capability
  - print-zone drawing and flat artwork dimensions
  - recommended decoration process

CLOSURE — please send:
  - exact supplier and closure product code
  - compatible neck finish
  - collar outer diameter and collar height
  - bulb dimensions, pipette dimensions, dip-tube length
  - closure technical drawing and material specification
  - finish and colour options
  - torque or assembly guidance
  - dimensional tolerances

COMPATIBILITY — please confirm in writing:
  - that the quoted bottle and closure are a compatible pair
  - assembled sample photographs
  - leakage-test information, and drop/transport-test data if held
  - recommended filling-line or assembly requirements

MATERIALS — please state the available options and tolerances for:
  - body: glass type, frosting method, colour/spraying system, durability, adhesion, chemical resistance, colour tolerance
  - collar: aluminium grade, anodizing or coating process, gloss level, surface treatment, corrosion resistance
  - closure: elastomer compound, hardness, colour options, compatibility with a cosmetic oil formula, chemical-resistance and migration data

DECORATION — please state which of screen printing, pad printing, hot stamping, foil, decal or UV printing you support on this item, together with maximum printable width and height, safe margin, wrap angle, any artwork distortion compensation you apply, colour count, registration tolerance, minimum line width, minimum readable text size, and your artwork file requirements.

COLOUR — for each decoration process you offer on this item, please state which colour systems you accept artwork in (Pantone, CMYK or spot colours), what you can hold to in each, and any colour tolerance you state.

Finally, please confirm MOQ, sample availability and lead time.

A dimensional drawing or CAD file is the single most useful item — our current geometry is a design concept and we will not treat it as production authority until your drawing confirms it.

Thank you.
