## WHAT `7ba1595` GOT RIGHT

**The count correction is the best thing in it.** I asserted four instances of
"extent correctly stated, population wrongly chosen." The sweep audited ten
extent statements, found two, examined a third and *declined to count it* on
the stated ground that including the root would not have changed the answer.
Refusing to inflate a count that flattered the brief's own thesis is the
discipline working at its best. D13 is opened at two.

**And D13 is right to be its own entry.** Not D10 — the choices were declared.
Not D12 — no fitted rule is involved. The mechanical signature is exact and
narrow: repository-root markdown excluded by directory-scoped greps, twice,
both times excluding the file that held the answer. The proposed control — make
root markdown the default population and require a reason to exclude it — is
about fifteen files' worth of cost, and it would have caught both. Naming it
honestly as a discipline with a cheap default rather than inventing a checker
that could not parse extent prose is the right call.

**The EAN check digit was verified rather than assumed** — computed 0 against
printed 0. Independently reconfirmed here: weighted sum 70, check digit 0. That
does not tell us whose barcode it is, and the record correctly does not pretend
it does.

**The reuse adjudication is item-by-item and the rulings differ**, which is
what I asked for and is the part most briefs get wrong by answering uniformly.
Splitting `ZHEZHUANG20210006` and the supplier identity to NO while leaving the
INCI list CONDITIONAL on a supplier question is exactly the right cut.

## THE DIE ANSWER IS BETTER THAN THE ONE I ASKED FOR

I asked "confirm or refute." The answer is **neither, and here is why** — the
label rects are stroked in the same near-black as the sheet boundary and the
general linework, not in the red the display box uses for its cut and crease
marks; `CUT_LINE_CANDIDATE=1` counts only the box contour; registration ink is
unused; MuPDF flattens layers. Nothing in the file distinguishes those rects as
die lines *or* as printed keylines.

That is a real UNDETERMINED rather than a shrug, and it carries a consequence I
had not seen: **if the near-black rects are the cut paths, T2's printed keyline
rides at the die edge and duplicates a cut visual.** T2 was already the
expensive treatment. It may also be the redundant one.

## AND I WAS WRONG ABOUT ALIGNMENT TWICE, IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS

In M7c I argued *for* ranged-left from the sheet — the common left edge making
twelve labels read as one system. In M7d I argued it was *undecidable*, because
the die destroys that edge. Both wrong, and wrong for the same underlying
reason: I kept treating the label as an element in a larger composition.

`7ba1595` puts it correctly in one clause — alignment is **a per-label question
under every die reading.** How a name sits inside its own 77 × 16 mm frame does
not depend on what happens to the twenty-three other frames, and it is free in
both ink and tolerance. It is DECIDABLE NOW, and it is the only one of the four
that is.

I implied the opposite in the M7d closing. That was my error and it is on the
record now rather than quietly corrected.

## WHAT COMES BACK TO YOU

**A single decision instead of four.** Right now the die, the stock, the
formulation and the case are four separate stalls in four separate records. If
Step 1 holds, they are one email and one caliper session, and you can weigh
"do I contact this vendor" once rather than working around it four times.

**A short, accurate email you can send or not send.** The one on file would
have told a vendor you cannot measure their own drawing, which stopped being
true the day the vector walk ran.

**And an honest ceiling.** Step 4 asks what M7 can still reach if nobody
replies and no calipers come out. I would rather you knew the height of that
ceiling before deciding whether to raise it.

**The one decision that is yours today is alignment** — centred or ranged left,
T1 against T4, free in both ink and tolerance and independent of everything
above. It is the only one of the four treatment variables that does not wait
on somebody else.
